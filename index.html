About

AI is changing how people work, learn and run their businesses. We think everyone should be able to understand it and use it, not just people who work in tech.

AI Horizons Society is a nonprofit organization incorporated in Delaware. We bring people together once a month to learn about AI. No tech background is needed.

Who it’s for

  • 01

    Anyone curious about AI

    You’ve heard about AI and want to understand what it is and what it can do. Start wherever you are.

  • 02

    Small-business owners

    You run a business and want to see where AI could fit into your day-to-day work.

  • 03

    Students and job seekers

    You’re building skills for your next role and want to get comfortable with AI tools.

Monthly meetings
